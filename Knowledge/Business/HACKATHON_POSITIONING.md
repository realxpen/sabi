# Hackathon Positioning

Status: ACTIVE

## Problem statement

Getting a product or service from the informal/local economy often requires fragmented manual coordination: asking friends, checking social media, sending messages, calling multiple people, checking availability, comparing prices, and deciding whom to trust.

## SABI answer

SABI turns that coordination problem into an agentic mission.

The user tells SABI:

- what they need
- budget
- location
- deadline
- important preferences

SABI can then find relevant providers, contact them, verify current information, compare qualifying options, and return the best choices while keeping the human in control of important decisions.

## Why it fits an agentic build event

The product naturally combines:

- reasoning
- tool use
- APIs/webhooks
- telephony
- messaging
- speech/language capability
- real-world human interaction
- structured observations
- repeated agent reasoning
- human approval

This is not merely a chat interface.

## Strong framing

> An AI interface for an economy that does not always have APIs.

## Hackathon pitch

Today, getting something from the informal economy often means searching Instagram, asking friends, checking WhatsApp and calling several people just to find someone available at the right price.

SABI changes that.

You tell SABI what you need, your budget, location and deadline. It finds relevant providers, contacts them, verifies price and availability, compares the responses and brings the best options back to you.

Using agentic AI, voice, messaging and African-language technology, SABI is exploring what it means to create an AI interface for an economy that does not always have APIs.

## Business-value story

SABI can potentially reduce:

- search time
- coordination overhead
- repeated calls/messages
- uncertainty about availability
- comparison effort

For providers, the longer-term opportunity is access to AI-mediated demand without requiring every small business to build sophisticated software first.

## Product discipline

The hackathon is a prototype of the primitive:

> Give AI a goal involving real people, constraints and communication, and let it coordinate the work safely.

Do not dilute the demo with ten unrelated use cases.
