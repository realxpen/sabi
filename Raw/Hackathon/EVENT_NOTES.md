## Organizer framing

The event is designed around building end-to-end agentic products, not only chat interfaces. Example capabilities described by organizers include agents that understand customers, reason over business data, take action, send messages, make real phone calls, speak African languages, and connect through APIs/webhooks.

Organizers encourage combining partner technologies into complete experiences.

## Partner capabilities described in organizer material

### KrosAI

AI-native telephony infrastructure for connecting AI agents to real phone numbers for inbound/outbound calls.

### Temlio

Communications infrastructure across Voice, SMS, USSD, and local virtual numbers.

### YarnGPT

Voice AI for African languages including English, Naija Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, and Hausa.

### Spitch

Speech APIs/SDKs for African languages, including speech-to-text and text-to-speech capabilities.

### BimpeAI

Agent platform described as supporting agent instructions/knowledge, tools/APIs, workflows, webhooks, deployment channels, logs, and conversations.
